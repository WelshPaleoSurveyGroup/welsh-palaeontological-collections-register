# Source attribution

The register is an aggregation and research-normalisation project. Individual records should retain their source institution and source URL.

## Major sources currently represented

### Amgueddfa Cymru – Museum Wales

- Collections Online: https://museum.wales/collections/online/
- Palaeontology holotype/lectotype database: https://museum.wales/database/geology/palaeontology/

### Natural History Museum, London

- NHM Data Portal: https://data.nhm.ac.uk/

### GB3D Type Fossils Online

- https://www.3d-fossils.ac.uk/

GB3D exposes type specimen records and digital imagery from multiple partner institutions. Rights/licences for images and models should be checked on the source record.

### Sedgwick Museum of Earth Sciences

- https://sedgwickmuseum.cam.ac.uk/collections/palaeontology/

### British Geological Survey

- National Geological Repository: https://www.bgs.ac.uk/geological-data/national-geological-repository/

### Grosvenor Museum

- https://www.cheshirewestmuseums.org/

## Publications

Primary descriptive and revisionary literature is cited in the workbook. Publication metadata and specimen evidence should be kept distinct: the paper is evidence for specimen interpretation, while the museum catalogue is evidence for current repository metadata.
