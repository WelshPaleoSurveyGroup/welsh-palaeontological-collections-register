To the extent that the project contributors hold rights in the original selection, arrangement, normalisation, and project-created metadata of the Welsh Palaeontological Collections Register, that material is intended for open scholarly reuse with attribution.

## Third-party source material

This repository aggregates and links to information supplied by museums, databases, publishers, researchers, and other institutions. **Third-party rights and source licences remain in force.**

In particular:

- museum photographs and scans are not automatically covered by this repository's licence;
- 3D models may have separate licences;
- publisher figures and article PDFs remain subject to their own copyright terms;
- catalogue metadata may be governed by the source institution's terms or open-data licence;
- links do not imply that the linked content may be redistributed.

Users should consult the original source before reproducing images, scans, models, or substantial third-party datasets.

## Attribution

When reusing records, please cite both this register and the original museum/database/publication source associated with the record.
