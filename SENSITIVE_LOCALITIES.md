# Sensitive locality policy

The internal research register may contain precise locality information needed for provenance research. Public versions should not automatically expose every coordinate.

A locality should be considered for generalisation or suppression when it is:

- vulnerable to collecting pressure;
- on private land;
- subject to access restrictions;
- a protected geological or biological site;
- an active research locality;
- supplied under an expectation of confidentiality;
- precise enough that publication could create conservation or landowner problems.

Recommended public statuses:

- **Public** — precise location may be shown.
- **Generalised** — show village, county, coastline, or broader locality only.
- **Restricted** — do not publish exact coordinates.
- **Embargoed** — withhold locality details until a stated condition is met.

The public database should never be treated as a collecting guide.
