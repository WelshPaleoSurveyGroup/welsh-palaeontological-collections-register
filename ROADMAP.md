# Roadmap

## Near term

- Complete systematic Museum Wales palaeontology type sweep.
- Harvest ordinary non-type Welsh records from Museum Wales.
- Expand NHMUK searches locality by locality.
- Continue GB3D Welsh type specimen sweep across partner institutions.
- Resolve duplicate/conflicting type status records.
- Normalise geological-unit aliases created during early passes.
- Add public-safe CSV exports.

## Medium term

- Oxford University Museum of Natural History audit.
- Sedgwick Museum direct catalogue/curatorial audit.
- Cambridge fossil vertebrate audit where relevant.
- BGS/National Geological Repository systematic Welsh search.
- Regional museums in Wales and neighbouring counties.
- University collections.
- Historical collector and collection-transfer research.
- Citation chaining through Google Scholar, Web of Science, and Scopus.

## Long term

- Coverage assessment by county, geological period, taxonomic group, and repository.
- Digitisation/3D coverage analysis.
- Historical collecting-bias analysis.
- Identification of specimens cited historically but now unlocated.
- Versioned public releases through GitHub and archival DOI deposition (e.g. Zenodo).
- Potential data paper or collections-infrastructure publication.
